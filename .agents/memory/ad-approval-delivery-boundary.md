---
name: Ad approval delivery boundary
description: Campaign activation and placement changes caused by ad review.
---

Approving or resuming an ad should add its selected placement to an eligible campaign and activate only draft or pending campaigns. Do not auto-resume a paused campaign on approval; only an explicit resume action can clear that pause. Never reactivate a suspended advertiser or bypass schedules, targeting, frequency limits, or emergency controls.

**Why:** Approval should remove redundant campaign setup without weakening intentional pauses or delivery safety controls.

**How to apply:** Preserve this boundary in advertising review, retry, and delivery-eligibility changes.
